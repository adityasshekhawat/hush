Do I need Claude to use it?

No. The character, meeting nudges, the wardrobe and rare finds work on their own. Agent alerts need Claude Code or Codex installed and use their hooks, with no model calls. Ask Hush answers and Summarise use your Claude Code login.

What does it read?

Your calendar, whether you're typing or on a call, and your Claude Code and Codex sessions, all on your Mac. The agent hooks send Hush only the event, session id, folder, host app and terminal device. What leaves your Mac

Does it cost anything?

Hush is free and open source. Ask Hush runs on your own Claude Code login, so answers count toward your Claude usage. History on your Mac shows what each one cost.

Will it show up when I share my screen?

Not by default. Hush's windows are left out of screen shares, recordings and screenshots. It's a setting, and it starts on.

Will it do things without asking?

No. Nothing is pasted, sent or run in another app on its own. Browser tasks are a beta you turn on, and they're told to ask before sending, buying or deleting.

How do I install it?

There's no signed download yet, so you build it from source: clone the repo and run ./scripts/bundle.sh. You need macOS 15 or later and Xcode or the Command Line Tools.
