# The Hush name and logo

The code is open (Apache-2.0) and so is the character art (CC BY-SA 4.0). The **name "Hush", the logo, the wordmark,
the app icon and the menu bar glyphs** (everything in `assets/brand`) are not, so that people can tell the official
app apart from other builds.

**You can:**
- build, run and share unmodified copies of Hush and call them Hush
- say your project is "based on Hush" or "a fork of Hush"
- use the name to talk about Hush, in articles, videos, reviews and so on

**Please don't:**
- release a modified version under the name Hush, or with the Hush logo or app icon. Give your fork its own name and
  icon (the character art is yours to reuse under its licence)
- suggest that your project or company is made or endorsed by Hush

Not sure? Open an issue and ask.
