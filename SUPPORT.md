# Getting help with Hush

- **Something's wrong?** Open an [issue](https://github.com/adityasshekhawat/hush/issues/new/choose) with what you did,
  what Hush did, and what you expected. `~/Library/Application Support/Hush/diagnostics.log` usually says why (read it
  before you paste it: it can name your projects and meetings).
- **A question?** Ask in [Discussions ▸ Q&A](https://github.com/adityasshekhawat/hush/discussions/categories/q-a).
- **An idea?** Open an issue with the idea template, or start a thread in
  [Ideas](https://github.com/adityasshekhawat/hush/discussions/categories/ideas).
- **Love your Hush?** Show it off in [Show and tell](https://github.com/adityasshekhawat/hush/discussions/categories/show-and-tell).
- **A security problem?** Please don't open an issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Hush is built and looked after in spare time, so replies can take a few days. Thanks for your patience.
