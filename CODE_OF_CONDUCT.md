# Code of conduct

Hush is about respecting people's attention. We'd like this project to respect people too.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in Hush a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and
healthy community.

## What we expect

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are new
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Owning our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them
- Focusing on what's best for the people who use Hush, not just for us

## What isn't welcome

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Where it applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and commits) and anywhere someone is officially
representing Hush.

## Reporting

Report privately, through GitHub:

1. **To the maintainer:** open the private reporting form at
   [Security ▸ Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/adityasshekhawat/hush/security/advisories/new) and start the
   title with **"Conduct:"**. Only the maintainer can see it. Include links to what happened and anything else that
   helps.
2. **To GitHub:** use **Report content** from the "…" menu on any comment, issue or pull request. That reaches
   GitHub's own moderators, under GitHub's [Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines).

Every report is reviewed and answered as quickly as possible. The privacy and security of the person reporting will
be respected.

## What happens next

The maintainer decides on the response, in proportion to what happened:

1. **Correction:** a private note explaining what was wrong, and possibly a request for a public apology.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with the people involved for
   a while.
3. **Temporary ban:** no interaction with the project for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban:** no further interaction with the project.
