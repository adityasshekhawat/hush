## Reserved: Hush itself

- **The Hush character:** its body shapes (blob, bean, square), face, eyes, expressions, poses, rig and animations,
  and the palettes as its look. Files: `assets/character/rig/`, `poses/`, `expressions/`, `states/`,
  `animations/`, `palettes.json`.
- **The name "Hush", the logo, the wordmark, the app icon and the menu bar glyphs:** `assets/brand/`.
- **Copies of these anywhere else in the repository,** such as the pack generated into `site/hush-data.js`, the
  renders in `docs/media/`, and screenshots or videos of the character.

## Open: everything that isn't Hush's original shape

**Wardrobe pieces, props, effects and edge pieces** are open under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](assets/character/swag/LICENSE). Files: `assets/character/swag/`,
`assets/character/edge/` and `assets/ui/`. Remix them, reuse them, and **contribute new ones:** hats, outfits,
held props, buddies, seasonal pieces, flags, doors, signs. Draw them to fit Hush's bodies (the rig is there to
draw against), but don't change the bodies, faces or poses themselves. Contributions here are under CC BY-SA 4.0
too, and you keep your copyright.

## What you can do

- Build and run Hush from this repository, as it is, and share unmodified builds under the name Hush.
- Fork it and contribute back with pull requests, including new art as described above.
- Show, review, write or talk about Hush, including screenshots and recordings of the character.

## What needs written permission

- Publishing an app, a fork or any other work that uses the Hush character (redrawn, traced or modified), the name
  "Hush", the logo or the app icon.
- Using any of them commercially, or in a way that suggests your project is Hush or is made or endorsed by it.

Forking Hush to ship your own app is welcome under the Apache License: give it **your own name, icon and
character**. The open wardrobe art can come with you.
